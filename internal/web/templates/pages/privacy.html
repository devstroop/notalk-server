Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use NoTalk, we collect:

  • Account information — your username, email address, and password (hashed).
  • WhatsApp session data — connection credentials necessary to maintain your WhatsApp sessions.
  • Messages and media — content sent and received through the platform as part of normal operation.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the Service (e.g., pages visited, features used).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Deliver messages and media through connected WhatsApp accounts
  • Send you important service updates and notifications
  • Improve the Service based on usage patterns

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the safety and security of our users
  • With your explicit consent

5. Message Content

Messages sent and received through NoTalk are processed to provide the Service. We do not read, analyze, or use your message content for advertising or any purpose other than delivering the Service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data

8. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the Service.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the platform.
