What NoTalk does

Everything you need to manage WhatsApp professionally, in one platform.

Multi-account management

Connect multiple WhatsApp numbers and manage them all from one dashboard. Each account runs independently with its own session.

WhatsApp Web

Send and receive messages, share media, react to messages, and manage groups — all from your browser. No phone needed.

REST API & Automation

64+ endpoints for complete programmatic control. Integrate WhatsApp messaging into any app, workflow, or service.

AI-powered assistants

26 MCP tools let AI assistants like Claude or Copilot read and send WhatsApp messages on your behalf.

Team collaboration

Invite team members with custom roles and permissions. Control who can send messages, manage accounts, or view data.

Real-time webhooks

Get instant callbacks when messages arrive. HMAC-signed, with retries and exponential backoff built in.

Built for every use case
